## 🎥 Video Overview

## ⚡ Super-Easy 1-Click Recording Method:
DealMind has a built-in **`🎬 60-Second Demo Walkthrough`** button in the top navigation bar!
1. Start your screen recording on `http://localhost:8080`.
2. Click **`🎬 60-Second Demo Walkthrough`**.
3. The app will automatically run the side-by-side memory battle, highlight citations, switch to the dynamic Knowledge Graph, and ask the Deal Strategist!
4. Simply read the narration script below as the app animates on your screen!

## 🎙️ Step-by-Step Script & Visual Cues

### Part 1: Quick Intro (0:00 – 0:30)
* **What to show on screen:** Your browser open to `http://localhost:8080` showing the **DealMind** dashboard.
* **Narration:**
  > "Hi everyone, I'm [Your Name]. In enterprise B2B sales, closing a single deal takes months and dozens of meetings with CFOs, architects, and security heads. 
  > The biggest reason sales reps lose deals is forgotten context—like a budget constraint mentioned in Call 1, or a competitor quote dropped in Call 2.
  > Today, I'm showing you **DealMind**, an autonomous deal intelligence copilot built with **Hindsight persistent memory** by Vectorize that allows AI agents to remember, recall, and adapt across an entire sales cycle."

---

### Part 2: The Problem Without Memory (0:30 – 1:00)
* **What to show on screen:** Hover over the **"Before vs. After Battle"** tab. Click on **"Standard AI (Stateless)"** or show the left column.
* **Narration:**
  > "Let's look at what happens with traditional, stateless AI. 
  > Here's our account, **Apex Logistics Solutions**, a $65,000 deal. 
  > If a rep asks a standard AI: *'Prepare my closing pitch for Monday's executive call'*, look at what it outputs:
  > It cheerfully tells us: *'Offer a 15% discount for an upfront annual contract!'*
  > Why is that a disaster? Because three weeks ago in Discovery, their CFO Sarah explicitly said they have an ironclad Q4 budget freeze and will veto any upfront cash commitments. The stateless AI just blew our deal because it has total amnesia."

---

### Part 3: The Live Demo With Hindsight (1:00 – 2:20)
* **What to show on screen:** 
  1. Click **"🧠 Hindsight Memory Explorer"** tab. Scroll through the timeline showing Call 1, Call 2, and Call 3 with tagged badges (`#objection:budget`, `#competitor:cloudx`).
  2. Switch back to the **"⚡ Before vs. After Battle"** tab and click **"🎯 Prepare Executive Closing Pitch"**.
  3. Watch the green right column populate and highlight the **Hindsight Memory Citations**.
* **Narration:**
  > "Now watch what happens when we enable **Hindsight Memory**.
  > First, in the Memory Explorer, you can see Hindsight has retained each interaction: 
  > In Call 1, it extracted Sarah's budget freeze. In Call 2, it retained Dave's refusal of vendor lock-in and a $15,000 quote from competitor CloudX.
  > Now, let's run the exact same prompt through DealMind:
  > Instantly, DealMind recalls the exact memories needed:
  > First, it warns us: *'Do NOT pitch annual upfront payment—propose a deferred-billing pilot starting in Q4.'*
  > Second, it neutralizes CloudX by highlighting our native Postgres connector and pointing out CloudX's hidden migration fees.
  > Third, it reassures Dave with our Docker export and open APIs.
  > Notice the citations at the bottom—it explicitly tells the rep which past call and quote each strategy came from."

---

### Part 4: Wrap-Up & Key Takeaway (2:20 – 2:50)
* **What to show on screen:** Briefly show the **"🎙️ Log Call & Retain Memory"** tab, demonstrating how easy it is to retain new transcripts, then show the GitHub repository.
* **Narration:**
  > "Logging new calls is as simple as pasting notes—Hindsight handles entity extraction and graph linking automatically.
  > The key takeaway is simple: **Persistent memory transforms AI from a cute conversational novelty into an indispensable enterprise partner.**
  > The entire codebase, architecture, and documentation are open source on GitHub. Thank you!"
